About

I'm a UX Content Designer with hands-on experience in user research, content design, and product design.

I currently write UX content for Microsoft's SharePoint team. Before that, I spent about 6 months on Microsoft Teams, writing for AI features like Recap, AI Interpreter, AI Signer, and Captions.

Before Microsoft, I spent 9 years at RCI driving content strategy and social media strategy. Outside my core role, I took the initiative to improve RCI.com's onboarding through UX writing.

I also took on freelance work for an edtech platform, where user testing showed me exactly where onboarding was confusing people, so I rewrote the content to improve it.

Alongside RCI, I've been part of a Bangalore-based NGO for years on a pro bono basis — I self-taught UI design and UX research to redesign their app from the ground up, worked with developers to bring it to life, and thought through the marketing promotion; it's now live on the App Store and Google Play. I'm still with them today, currently working on a redesign of their website.

As they say, focus on the user and all else will follow. It's a principle I keep coming back to. It shapes how I make decisions and keeps the user at the center of everything I design.
